Camera lenses

How to scan

      
    
        
  1. Enable all cameras. Every lens the browser exposes (wide, ultra-wide, telephoto, front) shows up as a tile. Each shot captures from all of them at once, so one press covers more of the scene and gives both wide context and sharp detail.
    2. 
        
  2. Move, then shoot. Change your position between shots by roughly 20–30° around an object or person, or step sideways about a metre in a room. Parallax from movement is what makes the 3D.
    3. 
        
  3. Overlap and texture. Each shot should share at least half of its view with the previous one. Plain white walls and glossy surfaces reconstruct poorly; patterned and matte surfaces reconstruct well.
    4. 
        
  4. Vary your distance. Take a few shots closer and a few further away. Phones do not tell the browser their focal lengths, and varying distance is what lets the app calibrate each lens.
    5. 
        
  5. Hold still. People should stay still while you walk around them. Avoid moving objects.
    6. 
        
  6. Build. Processing runs entirely on the phone. Fast quality takes under a minute for a dozen shots; High can take several minutes.
    7. 
        
  7. Export. Download GLB (opens in most 3D viewers and AR apps), PLY or OBJ. Scale is arbitrary because a single phone cannot measure absolute size.
